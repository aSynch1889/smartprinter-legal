Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026 · App: Smart Printer · Bundle ID: com.huazi.smartPrinter

This Privacy Policy explains how the Smart Printer iOS application (“App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Overview

Smart Printer is a wireless printing and document scanning utility. The App does not require you to create an account. We do not operate a user profile backend that collects personal data from the App for advertising or analytics tracking.

2. Information Processed on Your Device

Most data stays on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Documents & photos you choose to print or scan — processed locally to prepare print jobs or PDF files.
  • Print / scan history — file metadata and local copies may be stored in the App’s sandbox (Documents / local storage) so you can reprint or share later.
  • Preferences — appearance, language, onboarding state, free-usage counters, and similar settings may be stored using on-device storage such as UserDefaults.
  • Last selected printer — connection hints for AirPrint may be remembered locally to improve convenience.

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera — used only when you scan documents or similar content.
  • Photo Library — used only when you pick photos to print or layout.
  • Local Network — used to discover and communicate with AirPrint-compatible printers on your network.

You can revoke permissions in iOS Settings at any time. Without a permission, related features may be unavailable.

4. Web Content

If you use the in-app browser to open websites, those pages are provided by third parties. Their content, cookies, and data practices are outside our control. We recommend reviewing each site’s own privacy policy.

5. Purchases & Subscriptions

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit / App Store. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Apple’s privacy practices apply to purchase processing. Entitlement status may be verified with Apple’s systems and cached locally on your device for app functionality.

6. Data We Do Not Collect (Current Version)

  • No account registration email/password collected by the App
  • No advertising identifier–based tracking by the App
  • No sale of personal information

If a future version adds analytics, cloud sync, or account features, this policy will be updated before those features ship.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Local history and preferences remain on your device until you delete them in the App or remove the App. Uninstalling Smart Printer deletes its local data from that device (subject to iOS behavior and any backups you create).

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Third-Party Services

Printing uses Apple AirPrint and related system frameworks. Subscriptions use Apple App Store. Your use of those services is also subject to Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: 9366321@gmail.com

本隐私政策说明 Smart Printer iOS 应用（“本应用”“我们”）如何处理信息。使用本应用即表示你同意本政策。

1. 概述

Smart Printer 是一款无线打印与文档扫描工具。本应用不需要注册账号。我们不运营用于广告或分析跟踪的用户资料后台。

2. 在你设备本地处理的信息

  • 你选择打印或扫描的文档与照片 — 在本地处理以生成打印任务或 PDF。

  • 偏好设置 — 外观、语言、引导状态、免费次数等可能通过 UserDefaults 等本地存储保存。

3. 设备权限

  • 相机 — 仅在你扫描文档等场景使用。
  • 相册 — 仅在你选择照片打印或拼版时使用。
  • 本地网络 — 用于发现并连接同一网络中的 AirPrint 打印机。

4. 网页内容

若你使用应用内浏览器打开网站，页面由第三方提供，其内容与数据做法不由我们控制。

5. 购买与订阅

应用内购买与订阅由 Apple 通过 App Store / StoreKit 处理。我们不会接收或存储你的完整支付卡信息。权益状态可能经 Apple 校验，并在本地缓存以供功能使用。

6. 当前版本未收集的内容

  • 不收集应用内账号注册邮箱/密码
  • 不进行基于广告标识符的跟踪
  • 不出售个人信息

7. 保留与删除

本地历史与偏好会保留在设备上，直到你在应用中删除或卸载应用（并受 iOS 与备份机制影响）。

8. 儿童隐私

本应用不以 13 岁以下儿童为对象，我们不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。
