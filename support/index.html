Smart Printer for iPhone & iPad

Common questions

  • Printer not found? Make sure your iPhone/iPad and printer are on the same Wi‑Fi network, and that the printer supports AirPrint.
  • Cannot print? Open Settings in the App, choose a printer again, then retry. Restart the printer if needed.
  • Subscriptions? Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.
  • Restore purchases? Use Restore on the App’s upgrade screen.

常见问题

  • 找不到打印机？请确认 iPhone/iPad 与打印机连接同一 Wi‑Fi，且打印机支持 AirPrint。
  • 无法打印？请在应用设置中重新选择打印机后重试，必要时重启打印机。
  • 订阅管理？请前往 iOS 设置 → 你的姓名 → 订阅。
  • 恢复购买？请在应用升级页面使用「恢复购买」。
